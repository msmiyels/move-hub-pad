How to use a controller with the LEGO® Technic Move Hub

The Technic Move Hub (88019) sits in the LEGO Technic Porsche GT4 e-Performance Race Car (42176), the Lamborghini Revuelto Super Sports Car (42214) and the Batmobile Tumbler (42239). LEGO's CONTROL+ app has no gamepad support. Move Hub Pad is a free browser app that drives the hub with a PS5 DualSense or another gamepad over Web Bluetooth. No app, no account, no firmware change.

Open Move Hub Pad

What you need

  • A LEGO Technic set with the Technic Move Hub (88019), see the table below.
  • A gamepad. Tested with the PS5 DualSense; any other controller your browser detects should work too.
  • A browser with Web Bluetooth: Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux, or Bluefy on iPhone and iPad. Safari and Firefox do not support Web Bluetooth.

Step by step

  1. Pair the controller with your phone, tablet or computer in the Bluetooth settings.
  2. Open Move Hub Pad and press one button on the controller. Browsers hide a gamepad until it sends its first input.
  3. Press the green button on the hub until it blinks, tap Connect hub and pick the hub from the list. Do not pair the hub in the system Bluetooth settings.
  4. Press a button on the controller again once you are back on the page. The device picker takes focus and pauses everything.
  5. Keep the power limit low for the first run. 60 % is plenty indoors.
  6. Release the triggers and centre the stick to clear the safety lock, then drive.

Every input can be remapped in the Pad tab of the app.

Safety

  • Nothing moves until throttle and brake are released and the stick is centred.
  • The car stops by itself if the hub or the controller disconnects, the window loses focus or the tab is hidden. Keep the tab visible while driving.
  • The motors are strong enough to pinch and there is no mechanical kill switch.

FAQ

Does the LEGO CONTROL+ app support gamepads?

No. Only one app can hold the hub's Bluetooth connection at a time, so close CONTROL+ before you connect from the browser.

Is this for the LEGO BOOST Move Hub?

No. The BOOST Move Hub (88006) is a different, older hub. Move Hub Pad is made for the Technic Move Hub (88019).

Can I use Pybricks instead?

Not on this hub: its firmware is password-locked, so Pybricks cannot be installed.

Do I have to install anything?

No. Nothing is installed on your device or on the hub, everything runs in a browser tab.
